Why UNet++ for Dental Caries Detection?

Dental caries appear as subtle radiolucent shadows in teeth with high intra-class variance and faint boundaries. Traditional U-Net architecture suffers from a semantic gap between raw encoder feature maps and high-level decoder feature maps.

Key Architectural Advantages:

  • Nested Dense Pathways: Intermediate convolution nodes $X^{i,j}$ accumulate dense multi-scale context, preserving fine enamel boundary details.
  • Deep Supervision: Auxiliary losses at nodes $X^{0,1}, X^{0,2}, X^{0,3}, X^{0,4}$ force intermediate feature maps to be semantically discriminative.
  • Runtime Model Pruning: Allows choosing between ultra-fast screening ($L_1$) or full-depth precision ($L_4$).
